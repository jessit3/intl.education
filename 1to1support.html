Questions parents ask

What ages do you work with?

Roughly 7 to 17, and we assess each child individually. If yours is outside that range, get in touch.

Does it replace school?

That depends on your family. Some use it as their full home-education or worldschooling approach; others use it alongside a school that isn't meeting their child's needs.

Do you support neurodivergent and 2E learners?

Yes. Jess holds a GATE certificate for gifted and twice-exceptional learners, and James works with neurodivergent young people every day.

Are the digital tools safe?

Everything runs through managed Google Workspace for Education accounts, and the Gemini AI space is private and monitored. Using AI responsibly is part of what we teach.

What goes in the portfolio, and who sees it?

Everything your child makes. It can support local authority reviews, school or college transitions and future applications, and your family controls who can see it.

Can you invoice?

Yes. As a registered UK Community Interest Company we can issue invoices and supporting documents for home-education bodies, employers or grant applications.
