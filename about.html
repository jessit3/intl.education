Meet the founders

Two educators, one family, a lot of countries

Jess Marsh 🇺🇸 🇬🇧

Co-founder and director · Author of The Rebels' Handbook as J.R. Tyerman

Jessica is an international educator and social enterprise director with more than 20 years' experience educating learners of all ages. She obtained a Master's in Educational Psychology and Instructional Technology while working on a federal grant with her University of Southern California professors. As an instructor for her alma mater, she helped student teachers acquire credentials and infuse technology into their classrooms. She's lived in several countries, working in schools as a teacher, coordinator and librarian. She has conducted professional development in the US, Italy, Germany and the UK. She has blogged about her own worldschooling experiences.

Meanwhile, Jess has designed curriculum, a SaaS product, presented at conferences and contributed to educational articles and textbooks published internationally. As a Google for Education program manager, she advocated for educational organizations across the UK and Ireland to implement gamified and adaptive learning technologies. She believes that literacy skills (of all types) are essential to promote lifelong learning and has helped families, teachers and pupils achieve success through strategic support.

James Marsh 🇬🇧

Co-founder and director · Google Certified Educator (Levels 1 and 2)

James is a director of our community interest company (CIC) and a highly motivated practitioner of more than 15 years, striving to get the best from and for colleagues and pupils. He's a Google Certified Educator (Levels 1 & 2), utilizing digital technology to enhance learning experiences for all stakeholders, while equipping them with skills for the future. His passion for teaching along with love for edtech and sport has driven him to learn and teach others in a well-rounded manner.

He aims to inspire learners with high expectations yet nurture them to pursue their own discoveries. He embraces tools that help make learning accessible to all. With an ever-changing world within education, he has learned how to quickly adapt, demonstrating flexibility and leading by example. He has taught in the United Kingdom state (public) and independent (private) sectors as well as internationally.

Why we started

Jess knew early what it felt like to be underestimated, including the day a teacher took credit for a book she'd spent weeks writing and illustrating. Through two decades of teaching, she kept doing the same thing: standing up for capable young people other adults had written off too soon.

Then we became parents. Watching our son learn, so differently and so brilliantly, advocacy on its own stopped being enough. We started worldschooling, then writing about it, then built Worldschooling Quest, The Rebels' Handbook and the International Kids' Council.

Each one answers the same question: what happens when you actually trust a young person to lead?

What we believe

  • Every child's brilliance deserves to be seen, not dimmed or claimed by the adults around them.
  • Kids can lead real projects, with real voice and real money behind their ideas, right now.
  • Money sense, digital confidence and emotional strength matter as much as test scores.
  • Trust, not control, is what keeps a love of learning alive.
