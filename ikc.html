Questions families ask

Can we join now?

Not right now. The council isn't taking new members, but you can join the waitlist for our 12-week Respectful Rebels program.

How much time does it take?

One live video call a month, an optional monthly social, and offline tasks members choose to fit around their lives.

Is it only for gifted kids?

It's designed with gifted and high-potential learners in mind, but it's open to any child who finds rote learning dull. Neurodivergent learners who need context to engage often thrive here.

How do you keep members safe?

Each cohort agrees its own shared agreements through consent-based decision-making, and everyone, including facilitators, follows principles of honest, respectful speaking and listening. The community is moderated.

Does my child need tech skills?

No. We provide tutorials, prompts and support as they go.

Is it religious or secular?

Neither. Content is globally relevant and focused on skills, not on any set of beliefs.
