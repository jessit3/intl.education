Nine ways to be a Respectful Rebel

Which kind of Rebel are you?

Take the quiz

  • Advocate

    Notices who's missing from the conversation and speaks up so they're heard.

  • Inquirer

    Keeps asking "why?" until the real reason shows up.

  • Connector

    Spots what each person brings and links people together.

  • Trailblazer

    When both choices are bad, builds a third one: Option C.

  • Pathfinder

    Checks each choice against a compass: kind, wise, helpful, honest.

  • Innovator

    Turns complaints into "How might we...?" questions.

  • Catalyst

    Tests small and gets things moving; others join once it starts.

  • Explorer

    Looks for what's different and what's shared across places and people.

  • Changemaker

    Starts with one small action and lets it ripple outward.
