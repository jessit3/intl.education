Questions parents ask

Is this just for gifted kids?

No. It's for any young person who's bored by memorizing and thrives with more say over their learning. Many of our learners think differently, and that's welcome here.

Isn't this just more screen time?

It's screen time spent making, not scrolling. The project itself often happens away from the screen, out in the real world.

What if my teen doesn't engage?

Teens engage when the project is genuinely theirs. If you're unsure, book a call first and we'll talk honestly about whether it's a good fit.
